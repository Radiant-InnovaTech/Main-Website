# Illustration directions

These are proposals, not changes to the live site.

## 1. A real investigation, explained (recommended for the homepage)
Use the existing lab investigation screenshot. Show a readable crop with three short callouts: the alert, the evidence, and the verdict. On phones, stack the callouts below the screenshot and allow opening the full image. Label it as a lab investigation. Use actual displayed evidence and verdict text, without invented claims or customer data.

Why: visitors can see what Hydra produces, rather than trying to interpret a full dashboard reduced to phone size.

## 2. Follow the evidence (for How it works)
Use a simple navy and teal vector diagram: Alert → Questions → Evidence → Analyst review. Put each step in its own card, with short labels and a vertical layout on phones. Label this as the engine v2 design while v2 remains disabled in production. Avoid implying that the model decides confidence or operates outside the governed gateway.

Why: it explains the investigation without tiny text or decorative security imagery.

## 3. The analyst decides (for Security)
Show Hydra's findings entering an analyst review card, then a separate card for action in the organisation's own tools. Make the human decision the visible dividing point. Caption: “Hydra provides findings. Your team decides what to do.” Do not depict automatic attack closure or future integrations as current features.

Why: it makes the human control point easy to understand.

Use one visual style throughout: white backgrounds, navy text, teal connections, plain outlines, generous spacing. Keep real screenshots for product proof and use diagrams only to explain the workflow.
