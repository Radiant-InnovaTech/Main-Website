Talk to us

Write or call us. One of the founders reads every message and replies within two working days. If it helps, we will show you Hydra investigating a test alert, using data from our lab.

Our forms are switched off for now. We are not yet registered with Ghana's Data Protection Commission, and we will not collect your details through this website until we are.

To reach us, write to samuelmaclar@radiantinnovatech.com or call 050 455 5100. One of the founders replies within two working days.

What happens next

  1. A founder reads your message.
  2. We reply within two working days.
  3. If it fits, we suggest a call of about 30 minutes. You can ask us anything, including the uncomfortable questions.

We will not start with a customer until the Cyber Security Authority has confirmed our licence position in writing. We are glad to talk before then.

Other ways to reach us

  • Security teams: Samuel Maclar, 050 455 5100, samuelmaclar@radiantinnovatech.com.
  • Investors and partners: Samuel Maclar, samuelmaclar@radiantinnovatech.com.
  • To report a security problem: security@radiantinnovatech.com. See our Security page.
