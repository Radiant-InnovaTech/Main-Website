Be one of our first evaluation partners

Write or call us. One of the founders reads every message and replies within two working days. If it helps, we will show you Hydra investigating a test alert, using data from our lab.

What an evaluation is

  • Hydra reads your alerts for six weeks and writes down what it would conclude.
  • It changes nothing in your systems. Your analysts work as normal.
  • Every week we show you where Hydra and your analysts agreed and where they did not, and why.
  • At the end you get a written report and decide what to do next.
  • It is free for our first evaluation partners. If it is useful, we quote in writing afterwards.

We will not start with a team until the Cyber Security Authority has confirmed our licence position in writing, so we cannot give you a start date today. Joining now means we talk to you first.
